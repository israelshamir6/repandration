Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what Rep & Ration collects through Rep & Ration and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account: email, first name, password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile and logs: age, sex, height, weight, goals, diet and food exclusions, meals, workouts and weigh-ins.
  • Health data you choose to connect or import: heart rate, steps, blood pressure, sleep, stress, water.
  • Buddies: groups you create or join, and the phone numbers or emails you enter to invite someone (invites are sent from your own phone or email app).
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card number.

How we use it

Only to run the Service: build your plan, show your progress, send the health alerts you turn on, and share what you choose with your groups. We don't sell your data or use your health data for advertising.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host and operate the app (Netlify for hosting and database, Stripe for payments, our email delivery provider for password-reset emails), members of groups you join (only what you share), and authorities if the law requires it.

Health data

Health data is sensitive. We collect it only when you connect or import it, use it only to provide features you ask for, and delete it when you delete your account. If you live in California, you have the right under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) to know what personal information we collect, to request a copy, to correct it, and to have it deleted. Residents of Washington and Nevada have similar rights over consumer health data. We do not sell or share personal or health information. To make any request, email us.

Your choices

You can edit your data in the app, turn off alerts, disconnect devices, leave groups, and delete your account (Profile → Delete account), which erases your account and logs and cancels your subscription. To request a copy of your data, email Repandration27@gmail.com.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit, sessions use secure cookies and passwords are hashed. We keep data while your account is open and delete it when you delete the account, except billing records we must keep by law.

Children

The Service isn't for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We'll post updates here and notify you of material changes. Questions: Repandration27@gmail.com.
