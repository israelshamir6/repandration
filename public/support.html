Plans & billing

How much does it cost?
  • Monthly: $15.99/month
  • Annual: $120/year (that's $10/month, saving $71.88)
  • Family: $24.99/month or $199/year, for you plus 4 people
  • Coach: $29.99/month for up to 25 clients, or Coach Unlimited $49.99/month
  • Fleet & company: 20-seat minimum. $10 per seat per month for 20–50 seats, $8 per seat for 51 or more
  • Free: $0, no card. Log food by search, log workouts, follow your meal plan, track progress and join buddies

Sales tax is added where required.

Will I be charged during the free trial?

No. A card is needed to start the 7-day trial, but nothing is charged until day 8. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. We email you a reminder about 2 days before the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

Profile → Account & billing → Manage billing → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do you give refunds?

Payments are non-refundable except where required by law, but you can cancel any time before your next billing date and won't be charged again.

How do I switch to annual, family or coach?

Profile → Account & billing → Change plan. The switch happens right away and Stripe credits what's left of your current plan.

Food logging

Where does the food data come from?

Our built-in foods plus USDA FoodData Central (the U.S. government's nutrient database) and Open Food Facts (an open database of packaged foods from around the world). Together that's millions of foods, drinks, brands and barcodes.

How do I scan a barcode?

Tap + → Scan barcode and point your camera at the package. Allow camera access when asked. If the camera won't open, type the numbers under the barcode instead.

How does photo and "say it" logging work?

Snap your plate or describe what you ate ("2 eggs, toast with butter, coffee with milk"). AI lists the foods and estimates portions; you can untick items or change amounts before adding. Estimates are a starting point, so adjust if something looks off.

Can I save my own recipes and smoothies?

Yes. Food → New recipe, add ingredients from search, a barcode or a photo, set how many servings it makes, and save. Then log it by the serving.

Can I add a food that isn't in the database?

Yes: in the food search tap Custom food and copy the label. It's saved to your favorites. For a quick number, use Quick calories.

Calorie targets

How are my targets set?

We start with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation from your age, sex, height, weight and activity, then set a daily deficit or surplus from the weekly pace you choose (for example, losing 0.5% of your body weight per week).

What does "adaptive" mean?

Each week we compare what you logged with how your smoothed weight trend moved, estimate how much you really burn, and offer an updated target in a weekly check-in. It works best if you log food most days and weigh in 3 or more times a week. You can switch it off in Progress.

Why is my target raised to 1,200 or 1,500?

We don't set targets below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) without medical supervision.

Menus & diets

Which diets and practices are supported?

Any diet, vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, keto, low-carb, paleo and high-protein, plus faith and cultural practices: halal, kosher (meat and dairy kept apart), Daniel Fast, Lent (meatless Fridays), Orthodox fast days, Ramadan (Suhoor and Iftar), Hindu, Jain, Buddhist vegetarian, Seventh-day Adventist, Rastafarian Ital, Latter-day Saints and Sikh. Allergies and foods you leave out (fish, shellfish, eggs, dairy, gluten, nuts, soy, sesame and more) are removed from every menu.

Where do the recipes come from?

Every recipe is written for Rep & Ration. Many follow published guidance from USDA MyPlate, the NIH DASH eating plan, Mediterranean-diet guidance, the American Heart Association and the American Diabetes Association plate method. Nutrition is calculated from USDA data and scaled to your targets. Always check labels if you have a food allergy.

Can I plan to a budget?

Yes. In Plan, enter a weekly budget and tap Feed me on this. Menus switch to meals that fit, and you'll see cost per meal and a weekly total. Costs are estimates from US grocery averages.

What is "Shop your plan"?

Plan → Grocery & prep turns your week into one shopping list. Send it to Amazon Fresh in one tap, copy it, or share it.

Workouts

How many exercises are there?

Over 1,700, sorted into Home, Calisthenics, Heavy weights, Dumbbell, Kettlebell, Bands, Machines & cables, Stretch & mobility, Cardio & plyo, and On the road. Each has how-to steps, and most have a step-by-step demo. Demo images come from the public-domain free-exercise-db project, and videos from the wger project (credited on each exercise).

How does the app know when to add weight?

When you hit all your target reps on every set, the next session suggests a small increase (usually 5 lb on upper-body lifts and 10 lb on lower-body lifts). If you fall short, it keeps the weight and asks for one more rep.

Truckers & shift workers

What's trucker / on-the-road mode?

Pick Truck driver when you set up your profile (or later in Profile → Your day → On the road). Choose your route (over the road, regional, local or team) and what's in your cab (fridge or cooler, microwave or 12V cooker, blender). Local drivers get road meals by day and a home-cooked dinner; drivers with cab gear also get meals to prep at home and keep or heat in the truck. Add your DOT medical card date and Today counts down to your physical. Menus switch to food you can buy at truck stops, gas stations and delis, with no kitchen needed. Workouts become short cab-side, truck-stop and hotel-room circuits using bodyweight and bands.

I work nights. Can meal times follow my shift?

Yes. Choose Shift worker and enter your shift start and length. Meal times and reminders move with your shift.

Fleets & companies

How do fleet plans work?

Pick Fleets & companies on the plans page, enter your company name and number of seats (20 minimum), and check out. Then in Profile → Account & billing, add drivers' emails or share your join code. Each driver gets full Premium on their own private account. You can add or remove seats any time.

Can my company see my health data?

No. Your company sees your name, email, whether you've joined and when you last used the app. Your food, weight, sleep, blood pressure and DOT prep stay private to you.

My company gave me a code. Where do I enter it?

Create a free account (you can type the code on the sign-up form), or go to Profile → Account & billing and enter it under Join my company.

Free vs Premium

What's included free?

Logging food by search, logging workouts, your weekly meal plan, progress charts and buddies. Barcode, photo and "say it" logging, grocery lists, exercise demos and coach videos, the menu scanner, chain menus, cook-with-what-I-have, SNAP plans, DOT prep, fasting, sleep, water and steps, progress photos, the weekly AI review and reminders are Premium. Tap any of them and you can upgrade right there.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Once you've opened it, you can keep logging with no signal (handy on the road). Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

Family & coach plans

How do I add my family?

On a Family plan, go to Profile → Account & billing and add each person's email (up to 4). They sign up with that email and get full access with no card.

How does coach mode work?

Coaches get a code in the Coach tab. Clients enter it under Coach → Connect to a coach and choose what to share (food, workouts, weight). Coaches see a dashboard of each client and can message them. Clients keep their own membership and can disconnect any time.

App, reminders & sync

Is there an app?

Rep & Ration installs from your browser. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer, tap Install when it's offered. It opens full-screen like any app.

How do I get reminders?

Profile → Turn on reminders, then pick times for meals, workouts and weigh-ins. On iPhone, add the app to your home screen first and open it from there.

Does it sync with my Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch?

Automatic watch sync is coming with our native phone app, included in your membership.

Your data & account

Can I export my data?

Yes: Profile → Your data → download spreadsheets (CSV) or a full backup (JSON).

I forgot my password.

On the log-in screen tap Forgot password?. If the email doesn't arrive in a few minutes, check spam or email us.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account & billing → Delete account. This cancels your subscription and erases your data.
